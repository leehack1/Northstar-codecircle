GOOD QUESTIONS

It's okay to
ask.

A few answers to help you feel a bit more at home.

Still wondering? Say hello ↗
Do I need a referral to make an appointment?+

For most primary care and many specialist visits, you can book directly. Some insurance plans or specialist services may require a referral. If you're unsure, give us a call at (555) 014-8300 and we'll help you figure it out.

What should I bring to my first visit?+

Please bring a photo ID, your insurance card, a list of your current medications, and any relevant medical records. Arriving 15 minutes early gives you time to check in comfortably.

Which insurance plans do you accept?+

We work with many major insurance providers, but coverage depends on your plan. Call our billing team at (555) 014-8300 with your member details before your visit and we'll check for you.

Can I have an appointment by video?+

Yes. Virtual visits are available for many appointments. Request a visit using our appointment form and mention your preference when our team calls to confirm.

What should I do in a medical emergency?+

If you are experiencing a medical emergency, call your local emergency number or go to the nearest emergency department immediately. Do not use this website to seek emergency care.
